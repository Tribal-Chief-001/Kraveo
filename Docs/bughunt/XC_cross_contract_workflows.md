- Where: `routes/orders.ts:159-170` (pool = every PAID, unassigned ACCEPTED/PREPARING/READY order, no age limit), `orderFlow.ts:540-541` (`RIDER_BUSY` when the rider has any active order), `realtime.ts:211-224` (offers over the socket only to riders whose `dutyStatus` is exactly ONLINE; a rider holding a job is IN_TRANSIT via `refreshRiderDuty`, `orderFlow.ts:108-121`), `push/events.ts:97-107` (`idleRiders` excludes riders with an active order), `routes/orders.ts:425-499` (needs-attention).

## 4. Seam audit: what was compared and agrees (looked hard, found nothing)

- REST: customer `POST /orders` body/`clientRequestId`/error codes, `GET /orders` scope+cursor, `POST /payments/create-order` (`key_id/order_id/amount`), `verify-signature` (`PENDING_CONFIRMATION`, 409 `ORDER_CANCELLED` carrying `data`), `POST /orders/:id/cancel`, `POST /reviews` (`dishReviews[{dishId,rating}]`, tags, notes; `dhabaNotes` limits), `PUT /auth/profile` canonical `hostelBlock` echo; vendor status/reject/menu/items/vendors-status/location (`PUT /partner/vendor/location {lat,lng,accuracyM}` -> `data{lat,lng,locationSource,...}`), partner signup/login/me/application (`user`, `vendor`/`driver` blocks, `dutyStatus`, `rejectionReason`); rider claim/release/status/`verify-gate-otp {otpCode}`/duty/location; dashboard orders, reassign `{driverId,force}`, admin cancel/reset-otp/retry-refund, needs-attention (all 14 problem codes known to `orderProblems.ts`), applications/status/reset-password, vendor location PATCH, analytics fields, drivers, `/campus`.
- Enums/formats: order statuses (8), payment statuses (4), `refundStatus` NONE/PENDING/DONE/FAILED, `cancelledBy` CUSTOMER/VENDOR/ADMIN/SYSTEM, approval statuses and duty statuses are spelled identically on all sides; all timestamps are ISO-8601 UTC with `Z` and every client converts for display; money is rupee floats everywhere except Razorpay paise ints (compared server-side and re-checked in the customer app); ids are strings; order short code `#` + last 6 upper-case chars is the same in server push copy, customer, vendor, driver and dashboard; BH/GH names plus legacy normalisation agree (`config/campus.ts` vs the three app tables per CU2/VE/DR).
- Sockets: events `order_updated`, `new_order_alert`, `order_available`, `order_unavailable`, `rider_location`, `driver_location_update`, `driver_duty_update`, `partner_application`, `partner_application_updated` are emitted and consumed under the same names and payload shapes; `join_room` ack `{ok}` (plus `code RATE_LIMITED` when over 30/min) is read as `ok == true` by all four clients; the per-socket cap (30 joins/min, 10 order rooms) fits their use (one join per live order, vendor and rider re-join only after a reconnect or a refused ack, polls cover refusals); rooms and per-viewer `orderView` (OTP only to the owner at the gate and admins, rider location only to owner and admins) are enforced server-side.
- Push: payload `{event, orderId, v:"1"}` strings; `NEW_ORDER_REMINDER` travels as `NEW_ORDER` and is routed like it by the vendor app; channel ids `new_orders`, `order_updates`, `order_attention`, `new_deliveries` are created by the apps and default channel is `order_updates`; `app` enum and role check on `POST /devices`; vendor/driver/customer event sets match the server's `DEFS`; `REFUND_PROCESSED` is parsed by the customer app and only refreshes; `ORDER_CANCELLED` rules match Docs/18 section 9 (customer own cancel and vendor own reject not pushed).
- Realtime merge: server `updatedAt` changes on every write (Prisma `@updatedAt`, also `updateMany`), so `isNewerThan` / `isSupersededBy` / `isAtLeastAsNewAs` in the three apps and `upsertOrder` in the dashboard order the two cancel events (CANCELLED then REFUNDED) correctly; the customer app now keeps watching a cancelled order whose refund is pending (CU2-01 fix in `_needsUpdates`).
- Suspension: 401 `TOKEN_REVOKED` + `reason`, sockets closed, push tokens disabled, `drivers`/`vendor_` rooms dropped; vendor popups are closed by the session gate (VE-03 fix); driver shows the paused message.

## 8. First request after a PM2 restart (cold reconnect)

- Server: in-memory state is reset (login and sign-up throttles, admin lockout, join windows, auth cache); push in-flight work is lost but `sweepMissedPushes` re-sends missing NEW_ORDER/NEW_DELIVERY on the first 60 s tick (idempotent via `PushLog.key`); refunds left PENDING are picked up by the maintenance job; unpaid expiry and unaccepted auto-cancel run from the first tick.
- Sockets: customer (`order_realtime.dart:44-55`, delay 2-15 s), vendor (`order_socket.dart:64-74`), rider (`rider_socket.dart:101-112`) and dashboard (unlimited attempts, 1-10 s) reconnect on their own; each fresh socket gets a fresh 30-join budget, re-joins its rooms in the connect handler (customer all wanted orders, vendor `vendor_<id>`, rider watched orders) and then does a REST catch-up. Auto rooms (`user_`, `admins`, `drivers` if on duty, `vendor_` if approved) are decided at the handshake by two cheap queries per client, so a restart with a handful of devices is fine.
- Traps: events during the 2-15 s gap are lost (REST poll heals them: 15 s); the vendor alarm for an order paid during the restart comes from the sweep push; a customer in the payment sheet during the restart sees verify-signature fail with 5xx, shows "confirming" and recovers (webhook/reconcile). Restart BEFORE the demo (it also clears the admin passcode lockout and sign-up counters), not during it.
