Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Food is made fresh for each order, so we can’t take it back once it is being prepared. We do refund you in full whenever the order doesn’t happen or fails because of us or the restaurant. This page explains exactly when.

You get a full refund when

  • You cancel before the restaurant accepts your order. You can cancel from the order screen while it shows “Placed”.
  • The restaurant declines your order, for example because an item has run out.
  • The restaurant doesn’t respond. If a paid order is not accepted within 10 minutes, we cancel it and refund you automatically.
  • You were charged but the order failed, for example the payment went through after the order had expired, or you were charged twice.
  • Your order is not delivered, or Kraveo cancels it for any reason on our side.

We can’t refund when

  • You change your mind after the restaurant has accepted the order.
  • You don’t collect the order at the gate or share the gate code with the delivery partner.
  • The order was delivered as placed and you simply didn’t like the taste. Tell us anyway and we will pass your feedback to the restaurant.

Problems with a delivered order

If an item is missing, wrong, or the food is spoiled or unsafe, contact us right away with your order details. We review these case by case and, where the problem is confirmed, refund the affected items.

How and when you get your money

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank).
  • They are started automatically for cancelled orders. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show the amount.
  • If an order was never paid, nothing is charged and there is nothing to refund.

Contact us

Write to kraveo.contact@gmail.com with your order details. See also our Terms of Use.
