How it works

From craving
to doorstep
in four taps.

  1. 01

    Pick a kitchen

    Browse campus restaurants, add what you want, and tell us where you are — your hostel block, or any drop point if you're not a hosteller.

  2. 02

    Pay in a slide

    UPI or card through Razorpay, confirmed with a single slide. Your card and UPI details never touch Kraveo's servers.

  3. 03

    Watch it arrive

    The kitchen accepts, cooks and hands over to a rider. You see every status change as it happens, live on the map.

  4. 04

    Show the code at the gate

    Your rider arrives, you share a one-time code, done. No code, no handover — your food only goes to you.

Questions

Good
questions.

Where is Kraveo available?

We're starting at VIT Bhopal University and launching soon. We'll add more campuses once the first one runs smoothly.

When can I order?

Soon. We'd rather launch properly than launch early, so there's no public date yet. This page will update the moment the apps are live.

How do I pay?

UPI and cards through Razorpay. We never see or store your card number or UPI PIN.

I'm not a hosteller. Can I order?

Yes. Sign in with any Google account, say you're not a hostel student, and choose a drop point at checkout.

I run a kitchen or want to ride with you.

Partner accounts are created by us, not self-signed-up. Get in touch below and we'll set you up with a login for the Restaurant or Delivery Partner app.

Can I delete my data?

Yes — delete your account from inside the app. Read exactly what we collect and keep on the privacy page.
